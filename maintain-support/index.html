Maintain support

Maintain helps you stay near your target weight. It uses your food diary and weight trend to adjust your daily calorie target.

Get help

Open Settings → Feedback in Maintain or email t@tristanthomas.me. Include your iOS and Maintain versions and the steps that led to the problem.

Apple Health

Use Settings → Apple Health in Maintain to connect, reconnect, or change what Maintain writes. You can change which types of data Maintain can read and write in Apple Health.

Chat and meal estimates

Chat and cloud estimates need an internet connection. You can still enter food manually and use the offline catalogue. To turn off Chat and clear its saved conversation, open Chat, then choose Conversation → Turn off Chat.

Privacy and data requests

See the Maintain privacy policy for data controls. Email the developer for help accessing or deleting data you sent.
