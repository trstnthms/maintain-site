Maintain privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026

Maintain is provided by Tristan Thomas. This policy explains how Maintain processes your data to help you stay near your chosen weight.

Your diary and Apple Health

Your food diary, saved foods, goals, weigh-ins, and trend calculations are stored on your iPhone. Maintain works without an account. Device backup settings may apply to local app data.

Connecting Apple Health is optional. Depending on your permissions, Maintain can read weight, nutrition, and active and resting energy. It uses these to display trends and compare intake and expenditure. If you enable sharing to Apple Health, Maintain can write logged calories and macros and manual weigh-ins. You can change these permissions in Apple Health.

Chat with Maintain

If you enable Chat, Maintain sends your messages, attached photos, recent conversation context, and relevant results from your diary to OpenAI through Cloudflare. These can include saved or recurring foods, goals, weigh-ins, trends, logging dates and times, creatine records, and Apple Health settings, nutrition, weight, or Apple Watch energy data. These details help answer questions about your food, weight, and activity. Speech recognition runs on your iPhone and microphone audio stays on the device.

The saved conversation stays on your iPhone. In Chat, choose Conversation → Start a new conversation to clear it, or choose Turn off Chat to withdraw permission and clear the conversation.

Meal estimates and food lookups

When you request an online meal estimate or label reading, Maintain sends the description and photos you provide to OpenAI through Cloudflare. If you request an estimate while offline, it can be sent when you reconnect. The app may use public web sources to estimate a food or read a link you share. Online catalogue lookups send the barcode or product search term to Open Food Facts. On-device catalogue lookups use the downloaded database.

Maintain disables model response storage and configures its Cloudflare AI Gateway to disable payload logs and caching and use Zero Data Retention where supported. The service keeps an estimate result available for 30 minutes so an interrupted request can recover it. It clears expired results through scheduled cleanup. The service keeps usage records and request fingerprints for the current and previous three calendar months. Limited web-search traces, such as search terms, source paths, and outcomes, are kept for 30 days. Installation security records help authenticate requests. Inactive AI installation keys expire after 180 days.

Public food contributions

If you enable Contribute to Open Food Facts, eligible product corrections, barcodes, package details, and package photos you save can be published in the Open Food Facts database. Your private diary is separate from the submission. Public contributions can already have been copied by other users. Turning off the setting stops future submissions and removes pending contributions from Maintain. The service keeps a limited contribution receipt for up to 30 days. Open Food Facts terms of use.

Feedback and support

If you send feedback, Maintain sends your message, app and iOS versions, and attachments you choose to share. It uses a protected installation identifier to authenticate requests and show your feedback history. It strips location metadata from attachments. Feedback is stored privately with Cloudflare, and AI tools may help review it. Attachments expire after 90 days. The message and its status can remain after the attachments expire. If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the details you include.

How we use and share data

Maintain uses the optional Health and AI features with your permission. It uses limited service and feedback data to run, secure, and improve the app and respond to requests. Cloudflare provides hosting, storage, and AI routing, OpenAI processes AI requests, and GitHub hosts this website. These services may process data in other countries under their applicable data protection terms. Maintain does not sell your personal data or use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Data controls and your rights

Use Settings → Reset all data to clear the local diary and Maintain settings. Records in Apple Health, sent feedback, and contributions already published to Open Food Facts can remain. Manage Apple Health records in Apple Health and contact the developer about sent feedback or other privacy requests.

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and exercise other rights under applicable privacy law. If you are in the UK, you can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner’s Office.
